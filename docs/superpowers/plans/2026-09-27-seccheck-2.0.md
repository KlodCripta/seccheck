# SecCheck 2.0 implementation scope

Updated 10 October 2026 to follow the approved
[product design](../specs/2026-09-27-seccheck-2.0-design.md).

1. Remove the online maintenance module, its options, dependencies and tests.
2. Keep the three scanner boundaries and private evidence model.
3. Simplify the initial menu, scan result and follow-up choice.
4. Separate remaining items from improvements and explained warnings.
5. Add specific beginner explanations while preserving uncertainty and priority.
6. Apply the cold-white/red/orange/green palette with typography and title fallback.
7. Verify syntax, lint, behavioral and terminal flows; review before delivery.
8. Deliver a uniquely named development script for native testing. Keep main and
   the stable release unchanged.
